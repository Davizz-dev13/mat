Changes by M.A.T (2026-10-23) / cambios de M.A.T:
- Relabelled as a Leopard 2 (2A5/2A6 family): despite the original title, the geometry is a
  Leopard 2-type tank (wedge turret, seven road wheels per side). / Reetiquetado como Leopard 2.
- Remote weapon station (CROWS) removed. / Estación de armas remota eliminada.
- Split into parts for the exploded view (hull, engine deck, turret, gun, turret fittings,
  tracks, road wheels, drive wheels, side skirts); geometry simplified (about 126k triangles)
  and compressed with meshoptimizer; textures reduced to 512/256 px JPEG; materials retuned.
- Split into two files: leopard2.glb (hull, turret, skirts) and leopard2_tren.glb (running gear).
- Texture modified (2026-10-23, second pass): base-colour textures replaced by a uniform satin NATO green
  (as on the Spanish Army's Leopardo 2E) with ambient-occlusion shading and dust on the lower hull, skirts
  and tracks; the earlier camouflage, painted flag and ET plates were removed. / Textura modificada:
  verde OTAN satinado uniforme (como el Leopardo 2E del Ejército de Tierra) con sombreado y polvo.
- Roof leftovers of the original model (mast stubs, weapon-station base) removed. / Restos del techo eliminados.
- Stowage details added by M.A.T (built in code in the viewer, not part of the original model): NATO jerrycans,
  bustle basket with rolled tarp and kit bags, tow cables, spare track links, commander's periscope, two whip
  antennas with a Spanish flag and a NATO pennant, headlights and the yellow "70" bridge-class disc.
  / Detalles de estiba añadidos por M.A.T (bidones, cestón con lona, cables, eslabones, periscopio, antenas
  con bandera y banderín OTAN, faros y disco «70»).
